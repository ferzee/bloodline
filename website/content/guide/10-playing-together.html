Joining a house

  1. The head of the house types /bloodline invite <player>.

  2. The invited player chooses in the scroll that opens, or with the same buttons in chat (the invitation lasts 5 minutes):

    • [Join with your family]: keep your character and ancestors; they become a branch of the new house.
    • [Join as a newcomer]: start a new character in the new house; your old family's story ends.
    • [Decline]
  3. If you were the last member of your old house, that house ends, and its oath, renown and legacy are left behind.

Working together

  • On shared quests everyone's progress adds up: 12 kills from you and 18 from a friend finish "Slay 30 monsters". Whoever makes the final progress gets the reward.
  • Only the head of the house can invite, remove members, rename the house and swear the oath. The Members page in the Chronicle marks the head with a crown.
  • /bloodline leave takes you out of the house; you keep your character and found a new house. /bloodline head <player> passes leadership on. If the head leaves, the next member becomes head.
  • When another player kills you, your heir gets a blood debt against them.
