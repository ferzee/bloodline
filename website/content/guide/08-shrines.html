Finding a shrine

Shrines are small and fairly common: you'll pass one every few hundred blocks while exploring plains, forests, taiga, snowy lands, savanna, desert and badlands. They stand on level ground, never in a lake or on a cliff edge. When you step inside one, you hear its name, such as the Shrine of the Last Traveller. Every shrine keeps its name.

Once a day

Each family member can be blessed once a Minecraft day. Candles someone else already lit still count, so you can always pray. While you mourn, before choosing an heir, the candles won't catch.

A first visit

The first time anyone of your house prays at a shrine, the house gains 1 renown and the Chronicle records it. Every shrine counts once, so travelling the land adds up.
