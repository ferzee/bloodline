Resting placeVisit the spot where your ancestor died. Their dropped items are there too.3 levels

  • Blood debts pass down: an unpaid debt stays with the house when the next family member dies, and the house loses renown for it. A house carries at most 3 debts; when a newer one pushes out the oldest, that debt is forgotten.
  • Other duties end with the character who held them.
  • Track progress above your hotbar and on the Chronicle's Duties pages. Finished duties stay listed, crossed out, until the next heir takes over.

Renown

Renown is your house's standing. It is never spent: the oath and the steward's vault only check how much your house has.

✦

Where to see it

Your house's renown is on the first page of the Chronicle. Every change also shows in chat with the new total.
